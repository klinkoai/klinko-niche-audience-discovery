## What is Klinko Niche Audience Discovery?

Klinko Niche Audience Discovery is an AI market research skill for finding smaller, specific audiences that broad market analysis often misses. It helps teams surface underserved communities, emerging use cases, concentrated needs, and distinctive audience language so they can explore focused opportunities without defaulting to the largest or most obvious market.

## What this skill helps you do

- Break a broad category into specific niche audiences
- Surface overlooked or underserved audience groups
- Identify distinctive needs, contexts, and community signals
- Create a shortlist of niches for targeted validation

## Questions you can ask

- “What underserved niches exist in this market?”
- “Find overlooked audiences for my productivity app.”
- “Which small communities have a strong need for this product?”
- “What emerging audience segments should we investigate?”

## What you receive

| Deliverable | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| Niche shortlist | Select focused markets for exploration |
| Niche characteristics | Understand what makes each audience distinct |
| Unmet needs | Identify possible product or service gaps |
| Validation priorities | Choose which niche to investigate first |

## Example research process

1. Describe the product, idea, audience, or market you want to understand.
2. Add relevant public context such as geography, category, goals, or constraints.
3. Ask a clear decision question rather than requesting generic research.
4. Review the structured answer and use follow-up questions to refine the decision.

### Example prompt

> Discover underserved niches for a lightweight bookkeeping product aimed at solo service businesses. Focus on audiences with recurring administrative pain and limited specialist support.

### Example result structure

- Specific niche audiences rather than generic demographics
- The context that makes each niche meaningfully different
- Likely unmet needs and opportunity hypotheses
- A prioritized path for further validation
